# What Is EliteFreelancer?

It's a Nepal-focused creative services platform. Think of it as a place where clients can find and hire photographers, videographers, drone operators, and post-production folks. It's based in Kathmandu but serves people all across Nepal.

## What Kind of Services Can I Actually Book?

- **Photography:** Portraits, weddings, events, products, food, family, travel, and real estate.
- **Videography:** Weddings, commercials, documentaries, music videos, promos, events, and cinematic reels.
- **Drone Services:** Aerial photography and video for landscapes, real estate, travel, weddings, mapping, and inspection.
- **Post-Production:** Video editing, photo retouching, color correction and grading, graphics design, VFX, and motion graphics.

## How Does Hiring Work?

It's a simple four-step flow:

1. **Browse Services** — Explore freelancers by category and portfolio.
2. **Check CV** — Review a freelancer's portfolio and past client reviews.
3. **Contact & Hire** — Discuss project requirements directly with the professional and hire them.
4. **Get Results** — Receive the completed work and provide feedback.

### For Freelancers:

1. Create a profile.
2. Upload your portfolio and set rates.
3. Get verified.
4. Browse projects and submit proposals.
5. Deliver work and get paid plus reviews.

But those are promotional website figures. You can't get an exact price without a current package or booking calculation. So treat those rates as a ballpark, not a quote.

## Can I Book Something Same-Day?
